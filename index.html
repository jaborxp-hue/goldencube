About us

A family trading house built on quality and fair prices

Golden Cube General Trading Co. Ltd. is a registered Iraqi limited liability company. It carries forward the experience of the Al-Anwar stationery business in Nasiriyah, trading paper, school and office supplies since 1981.

What began as a stationery shop on Al-Habboubi Street has grown into a wholesale and retail operation with its own showroom, retail store and warehouses, serving bookshops and institutions across Dhi Qar.

For many years we have imported directly from manufacturers, travelling to China every year to visit factories and trade fairs. We work with long-standing production partners in Yiwu and Shanghai and plan each season around their production calendars.

Two principles have not changed since the beginning: we do not compromise on quality, and we keep our prices fair.
